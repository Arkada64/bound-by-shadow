Generators need two gas cans each, and you can carry one can at a time. More survivors means more generators: 4 with 3 others, up to 8 with 7. Drop pallets in doorways to block or stun the killer; run into a dropped pallet to vault it.

Your flashlight helps you see, and it helps the killer see you. Closed doors block sight. Lockers hide you, unless it saw you get in.

This prototype needs a keyboard and mouse.
